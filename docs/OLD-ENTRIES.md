# Adding old entries (the notebook, files, photos)

Open it from a customer's page (link at the bottom: "Notebook entries from before? Add old entries") or Reports → Add old entries. Nothing is saved until you check the rows and tap **Import**.

1. **Type from a photo or PDF (free).** Pick the page photo (or take one, or choose a PDF in the browser). It stays at the top (bigger / smaller / hide) while you type the rows below.
   - "+ Add row" copies the date to the next row. Enter the balance written at the top and at the bottom of the page: Munim re-adds everything by code and tells you if a digit is wrong.
   - An item that is not in your list: tap "+ Add “name” as a new item".
2. **Upload a spreadsheet.** A CSV file, or paste cells copied from Excel / Google Sheets. Columns it understands (English or Hindi headings): Date, Item, Qty, Unit, Rate, Amount (or Debit and Credit), Type, Mode, Site. With no heading row the order is Date, Item, Qty, Unit, Rate, Amount.
   If the sheet lists several customers, only the chosen customer's rows are used; repeat for each customer. ("Get a sample file" shares a template.)
3. **Opening balances only.** One list with every customer's old balance (purana baaki).
4. **Scan with AI.** Reads the photo for you: needs the AI reader switched on (docs/AI-SETUP.md). Cheap and one-time for a backlog, but on hold for now.

Safeguards: the same checks as a scanned page (balances re-added by code, items matched to your list, sites, duplicates of what is already saved are skipped). Imported entries go through the normal save path with their old dates.
